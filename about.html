Burlington News

Independent local news and civic information for Burlington, Ontario.

Why it exists

Useful Burlington information is spread across public records, City pages, school boards, organizations, reporting and community discussion. Burlington News brings the strongest sources together and explains what matters without adding unnecessary copy.

What it covers

Local news, elections, schools, events, food, sports, history and practical things to do. A social post can begin the reporting, but it is not treated as proof or permission to reuse somebody’s work.

Independent

The site is resident-built. It is not operated by the City of Burlington, Halton Region, a political party, candidate, campaign or public agency. It does not endorse candidates.
