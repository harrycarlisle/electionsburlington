Burlington News

Independent local news and civic information for Burlington, Ontario.

Why it exists

Useful Burlington information is spread across public records, City pages, school boards, organizations, reporting and community discussion. Burlington News brings the strongest sources together and explains what matters.

Ownership and funding

The site is resident-built by Harry Carlisle. It is not owned by the City, Halton Region, a chain or a political party. There is no outside newsroom owner. Partnerships and story pitches go through Work with us. No business pays for a news judgement.
