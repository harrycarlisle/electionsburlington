Credibility

AI policy

Direct answer

Software can check selected sources, remove duplicates and help draft a concise summary. It cannot invent quotes, reporter names, event dates or crime rankings. Burlington News remains responsible for what it publishes.

  • Uncertain facts are omitted or labelled, not guessed.
  • Live utility pages show scheduled or “no current sample” rather than a fake live number.
  • Editorial illustrations are labelled as illustrations.
  • Automated monitoring does not turn an unverified social post into an article.
