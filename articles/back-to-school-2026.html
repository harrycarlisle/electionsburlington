Back to school

The September school dates Burlington families actually need.

Students do not return until Tuesday, Sept. 8. The reason is a stack of PA days and Labour Day that makes the first week look more confusing than it is.

By Burlington NewsAugust 25, 20263-minute read

The date most families need to remember is Tuesday, Sept. 8.

That is the first day of instruction for students in the Halton District School Board's 2026-27 calendar. The later start is not because the school year has been shortened. Staff are already back before students arrive.

The first dates
  • Sept. 2: Elementary and secondary PA day
  • Sept. 3: Elementary and secondary PA day
  • Sept. 7: Labour Day
  • Sept. 8: First day of instruction for students
  • Oct. 9: Next elementary and secondary PA day
  • Oct. 12: Thanksgiving

Why this first week feels unusual

Two PA days land before Labour Day, which means staff preparation begins while students still have several days off. Then the holiday pushes the first student day to Tuesday.

For families, that creates one last long weekend to cover. For drivers, Sept. 8 is when Burlington's school-zone traffic, school buses and pickup congestion return all at once.

The date after Sept. 8 that catches people

The next system-wide PA day arrives Friday, Oct. 9, directly before Thanksgiving weekend. Families who need child care or work coverage may want to plan for that date before the September routine gets busy.

The board also schedules early-dismissal days later in the year. On those days, students are dismissed 90 minutes early, which can matter just as much as a PA day for pickup and after-school care.

One important limitation: this article covers the Halton District School Board calendar. Catholic-board and private-school schedules can differ, so confirm your own school's calendar before making plans.
