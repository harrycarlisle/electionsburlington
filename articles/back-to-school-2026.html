Back to school

The September dates Burlington families actually need.

The first week looks later than usual because staff preparation, Labour Day and the first day of class fall on three different parts of the calendar.

By Burlington NewsAugust 23, 20263-minute read

The date to circle is Tuesday, Sept. 8. That is the first day of instruction for students in the Halton District School Board’s 2026–27 calendar.

Before students return, elementary and secondary staff have PA days on Wednesday, Sept. 2 and Thursday, Sept. 3. Labour Day falls on Monday, Sept. 7. The result is a long gap between the end of August and the first morning of class, even though school operations are already restarting behind the scenes.

The first dates
  • Sept. 2: Elementary and secondary PA day
  • Sept. 3: Elementary and secondary PA day
  • Sept. 7: Labour Day
  • Sept. 8: First day of instruction for all students
  • Oct. 9: Next elementary and secondary PA day
  • Oct. 12: Thanksgiving

Why the calendar matters beyond school

The first day changes the city’s rhythm. School-zone traffic returns, buses stop more frequently and family schedules tighten at the same time many workplaces shift out of summer mode. Drivers should expect travel patterns around Burlington schools to feel different before and after Sept. 8.

The board also marks some end-of-term dates with early dismissal. On those days students are dismissed 90 minutes early. That detail matters later in the year, particularly for families arranging pickup or child care.

This article covers the Halton District School Board calendar. Catholic-board and private-school schedules may differ, so families should confirm dates with their own school or board rather than assuming every Burlington student follows the same calendar.
