Can I swim at Burlington Beach today?

A lab result is a Wednesday snapshot, not a promise about this afternoon’s water.

Direct answer

Halton Region samples Beachway and Brant Street Beach in season, usually weekly through Labour Day. This page only shows SAFE or NOT RECOMMENDED when an official sample date is on file. Right now the honest status is NO CURRENT SAMPLE. Use Halton’s beach-water page before you get in.

How to read a result

Ontario treats water as not recommended when the geometric mean of at least five samples is 200 or more E. coli per 100 mL, or one sample is 400 or more. Rain, waves and cloudy water can change conditions between the sample and the swim. After Labour Day, routine sampling stops.
