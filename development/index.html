What is changing

Burlington’s biggest developments, mapped

From Aldershot to Appleby, these are the major projects to watch, what’s approved and what happens next.

Planning approval does not mean construction has started.

Projects

1200 King Road

Location: Aldershot

Size: About 8,800 homes on 49 hectares. An entertainment node can include an arena.

Status: Approved July 2026. Holding provisions still apply.

Next: Those holding provisions have to be cleared, and more applications filed, before construction.

Source: City staff report

Aldershot GO TOC

Location: 119 Masonry Court, beside Aldershot GO

Size: About 2,713 units in buildings 30 to 48 storeys

Status: Provincial proposal. The City’s technical review is not finished.

Next: Traffic, servicing, and how it fits the station-area plan still have to be reviewed.

Source: City project page

3110 South Service Road

Location: QEW corridor

Size: About 55,000 sq. ft. The City calls it a general-purpose data centre.

Status: Active City file. The City has not described it as an AI hyperscale campus.

Next: Power, noise, and who pays for the infrastructure.

Source: Burlington News explainer

Aldershot, Burlington and Appleby GO MTSAs

Location: The three Lakeshore West stations

Size: Planned growth districts, not one building

Status: Station-area plans are in place. Individual sites still need their own applications.

Next: Watch each tower file as it lands.

Source: Official Plan

Millcroft Phase 2

Location: Former Millcroft Golf Course

Size: 138 homes in the current Phase 2 proposal

Status: Still under City review

Next: A planning decision on the 138-home application.

Source: What is happening to Millcroft?

Old Lakeshore towers

Location: Old Lakeshore Road, downtown waterfront

Size: Towers already approved. What gets built still depends on remaining work.

Status: Approval is not occupancy.

Next: Construction timing is different for each project.

Source: City development list

730 Brant Street

Location: 730 Brant Street

Size: Vacant building on a site already approved for redevelopment

Status: Empty after a fire. The redevelopment approval predates the fire.

Next: What the owner actually builds.

Source: 730 Brant explainer

Nelson Quarry

Location: Mount Nemo

Size: A quarry decision, not housing

Status: Ontario Land Tribunal decision issued

Next: The ruling is not the same as trucks starting tomorrow.

Source: Quarry explainer
