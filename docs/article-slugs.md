# Article slug policy

Headlines can be curiosity-driven. Public URLs should be descriptive and stable.

## Public form

Canonical articles live at `/stories/<slug>/`. Do not create a third article URL family.

## Rules

- lowercase
- hyphens
- short
- factual
- include the geographic entity when it is genuinely relevant
- name the topic or entity
- add a distinctive durable fact if it helps
- no filler words unless they are needed
- no clickbait claim that is likely to become false
- no date unless the date is essential to the content
- do not change a good slug every time a headline changes

## Example

Headline:

> This Burlington team is 0–24. Why do they keep coming back?

Stable URL:

`/stories/burlington-ultimate-team-0-24/`

Do not use a headline-shaped slug such as `/burlington-sports-team-has-never-won-a-game/`. “Never won” can become false, and “sports team” is too vague.

If a slug must change, permanently redirect the old public URL to the new canonical in one hop. Keep the old URL out of the sitemap.
