# Explore Burlington passport

Explore is a local discovery tool, not a completion trap. It should give one specific idea at a time, show where the idea sits in Burlington, and remember progress without asking for an account.

## Current experience

- Twelve stops appear in the shuffle deck, the full list and an orientation map.
- A single progress line fills as stops are marked explored.
- The endpoint represents a visible but locked bonus stop.
- Completing all twelve reveals the thirteenth-stop prompt and a Burlington Explorer badge.
- Progress and the badge remain in the visitor's browser through `localStorage`.

The map is an orientation aid, not a boundary or navigation map. Pins must use reasonable approximate coordinates, carry an accessible place label and link back to the corresponding deck card. Exact routes belong in the linked official map or place page.

## Why the bonus does not require a selfie

A selfie gate would require a clear purpose, consent language, secure upload storage, retention and deletion rules, abuse handling and a way to verify submissions without encouraging risky behaviour at a location. It would also make a light local challenge feel like an identity system.

The first release therefore rewards completion locally and invites an optional suggestion through the existing feedback route. It does not upload an image or create a public profile.

## Conditions for a future account layer

Accounts, photo proof and article comments should be treated as one separate product decision. Do not add them until the site has:

1. authentication and account deletion;
2. upload permission, storage, retention and reporting rules;
3. moderation tools and a published community standard;
4. rate limits, spam protection and an appeals path;
5. privacy and terms updates reviewed before launch;
6. a clear benefit that cannot be delivered with local browser progress.

A badge may become portable later, but an account should not be the price of discovering Burlington.
