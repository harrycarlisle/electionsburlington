# Burlington News publication checklist

The writer completes this list. A second pass—human or automated—must be able to reproduce every answer from the story file and reporting notes.

## Pitch gate

- [ ] The story has one clear narrative question.
- [ ] Burlington is essential to the story.
- [ ] The reveal is surprising because of evidence, not phrasing.
- [ ] The archive does not already contain the same story.
- [ ] The reporting can support the intended length.

## Reporting gate

- [ ] Every date, number, name, title, place and superlative has a source.
- [ ] Primary records were used where available.
- [ ] Community posts were treated as leads, not evidence.
- [ ] Crime, misconduct and allegations meet the two-source rule, including a primary or public record where one exists.
- [ ] Quotes are verbatim, attributable and in context.
- [ ] Inference is labelled as inference.
- [ ] Unknowns remain unknown; the draft does not fill gaps with plausible detail.

## Writing pass

- [ ] The opening contains a documented scene or fact and introduces the question quickly.
- [ ] The chronology is clear.
- [ ] Every paragraph adds evidence, scene, consequence or movement.
- [ ] Abstract phrases such as “shaped,” “underscores,” “speaks to” and “deeper challenge” were replaced where a fact can do the work.
- [ ] Contractions sound natural rather than artificially formal.
- [ ] Repeated conclusions and five-part template sections were removed.
- [ ] The ending answers the opening rather than announcing a lesson.

## Headline pass

- [ ] Five different candidates were written.
- [ ] The chosen headline scored at least 10/14 on `headline-rubric.md`.
- [ ] The deck adds a second fact or open loop.
- [ ] Both headline and deck remain accurate when read without the article.
- [ ] The mobile line breaks were reviewed.

## Visual and rights pass

- [ ] Each visual advances the story rather than decorating it.
- [ ] Copyright status, creator, source URL and required credit are recorded.
- [ ] No Google Maps, Street View, social-media or website image was copied without an applicable licence or permission.
- [ ] Generated or reconstructed visuals are source-anchored and prominently labelled.
- [ ] Alt text describes the useful content without repeating the caption.
- [ ] Image dimensions, modern format and file size are appropriate.

## Harm, privacy and fairness pass

- [ ] Living people were given a fair opportunity to respond to significant criticism.
- [ ] The story distinguishes allegation, charge, finding and conviction.
- [ ] Private details are necessary to the public-interest purpose.
- [ ] The language does not imply motive without evidence.
- [ ] The story is not more confident than its sources.

## Technical publication pass

- [ ] Internal and external links return successfully.
- [ ] Canonical URL, title, description, social image and structured data match the story.
- [ ] Search index, homepage/Latest, sitemap and editorial archive are updated.
- [ ] Desktop light, mobile light and mobile dark were visually reviewed.
- [ ] Keyboard focus, headings, landmarks and contrast were checked.
- [ ] The page was tested at 375 px, 768 px and desktop width.
- [ ] No render-blocking third-party font or unnecessary asset was added.
- [ ] The live URL was checked after deployment.

If a required box cannot be checked, save the work as a pitch or draft. Do not publish it to fill a schedule.
