## Publish-now story packets

### 1. SEKISUI modular factory

**Working title:** Up to 90% of a building can now be made inside this Burlington factory

**Core finding:** SEKISUI Canada Modular Solutions launched its Canadian manufacturing and operations hub in Burlington. Invest Ontario says the investment is $15 million and the process can move up to 90% of building construction into a controlled factory. SEKISUI’s parent-company plan says the Canadian operation is targeting 500–600 modules a year and initially plans low-rise schools and 4–8-storey residential / care buildings.

**Why Burlington News:** The press release says “investment.” The useful local story is what the factory actually makes, how much of a building can be finished indoors, and the scale the company says it is targeting.

**Primary sources:**
- Invest Ontario, Aug. 19, 2026: https://www.investontario.ca/press-release/invest-ontario-welcomes-sekisui-canada-modular-solutions-expansion-burlington
- SEKISUI Heim parent-company release, Dec. 11, 2025: https://www.sekisuiheim.com/info/press/20251211.html
- SEKISUI CMS official launch listing confirms 955 Maple Ave.: https://luma.com/6w83woch

**Reporting still useful:** first Canadian customer/project, current headcount, precise factory floor area, whether 500–600 modules remains the current annual target.

**Visual:** original Burlington News “factory → module → building” diagram. Do not imply it depicts an actual SEKISUI project.

### 2. Costco near Burloak

**Working title:** Oakville approved a 1,000-space Costco near Burloak. Here is how drivers will get in

**Core finding:** Oakville lists the Wyecroft Costco among recently approved site plans. The current plan calls for 15,662.6 m² of gross floor area, 1,000 vehicle spaces, a gas bar, two Wyecroft access points feeding underground parking, plus two RioCan Boulevard access points.

**Why Burlington News:** The store is in Oakville, but it sits in the Burloak/Wyecroft area immediately relevant to east Burlington drivers. The value-add is status + site access + traffic context, not another “Costco is coming” rewrite.

**Primary sources:**
- Oakville Costco application: https://www.oakville.ca/business-development/planning-development/active-development-applications/ward-1-development-and-site-plan-applications/costco-oakville-%28wyecroft%29-3471-3487-3505-3509-3513-3517-and-3521-wyecroft-road-1635-031-01/
- Oakville Ward 1 application index, listing it as recently approved: https://www.oakville.ca/business-development/planning-development/active-development-applications/ward-1-development-and-site-plan-applications/
- Current official site plan: https://www.oakville.ca/getmedia/f9604943-aff0-4399-8ef2-a25f2783cb5c/sp-163503101-SitePlan-S2.pdf
- Burloak grade-separation project: https://www.oakville.ca/transportation-roads/construction-projects/roads-bridges-storm-sewers/burloak-drive-grade-separation/

**Reporting still useful:** building-permit status, a Costco-confirmed opening date, current construction milestone, full transportation-study trip-generation assumptions.

**Visual:** simplified Burlington News access diagram based on the official site plan. Label it as a schematic, not a surveyed plan.

### 3. Road closures

**Working title:** The Burlington road closures still in effect heading into September, mapped

**Core finding:** As of Aug. 26, two clearly significant full City road closures have confirmed end dates beyond today: Spruce Avenue between Shoreacres Road and Goodram Drive through Sept. 4 for a watermain, and Britannia Road between Walkers Line and Guelph Line through the end of September for a culvert replacement. ## High-value next research

### Flooding

Current primary-source findings are unusually strong:
- Burlington says it is investing roughly $7.9M–$8M in stormwater projects in 2026 and about $90M since 2015.
- Tuck Creek work is a direct response to Aug. 4, 2014 overtopping at New Street, Regal Road and Spruce Avenue. Regal was completed in 2019; New Street bridge work is scheduled 2025/26; Spruce is scheduled 2027/28.
- Lower Rambo Creek has an active flood-mitigation EA; a draft report was under City review in Feb. 2026.
- Halton Region says field investigations cover 268 km of wastewater mains across 11 priority areas where 90% of surcharge-related flooding occurred region-wide.

Do not turn region-wide priority-area figures into Burlington-specific neighbourhood claims without the underlying geography.

### Development transformation list

Use existing `data/developments.json` as the base but do not duplicate the tracker. Strong additional candidates from current City records include:
- Bronte Creek Meadows Phase 1: approved; 254 lots/blocks plus parks, employment, natural heritage, stormwater and new roads.
- 2076 Old Lakeshore Road: approved; 23 storeys, 50 residential units, 154 hotel rooms and waterfront-trail extension.
- 1056–1060 Brant Street: under review; 11 storeys, about 93 units.
- 198/200/206 Plains Road East: under review; 7 storeys, 66 units plus ground-floor commercial.

A “five-year confidence” methodology should heavily reward approvals / active site work and explicitly discount early proposals.

### Forgotten Burlington

The City’s history page confirms modern Burlington absorbed or grew around historic places including Wellington Square, Port Nelson, Nelson, Appleby, Zimmerman, Lowville and Kilbride. Burlington Public Library holds Nelson and Aldershot Tweedsmuir history collections. Best next move is one community with a strong visual transformation, not a listicle.

### Made in Burlington

Discover Burlington lists major advanced-manufacturing employers such as Evertz Microsystems and other technology/manufacturing firms, but employer presence alone is not a story. Find products with a documented Burlington design/manufacturing origin and global installation count / customers / geographic reach.

- **Intersections:** obtain intersection-level collisions, severity, volumes and ideally public complaint/correspondence data before ranking “complained about most.”
- **Ontario changes:** revisit closer to Sept. 21. Confirmed Residential Tenancies Act provisions take effect that day; do not pad to six.
- **Project-budget / cost-overrun stories:** scan FIN-10-26 capital variance and individual project reports for one clear case with original vs current scope/cost.
- **Traffic intervention:** choose an intervention with at least 12 months of before/after data.
- **Councillor before/after:** wait for a consequential vote where positions genuinely move.
- **Street names:** use Burlington History Room / heritage records for origins; do not copy folklore.
- **Small-park vote:** define eligibility and Reader’s Choice rules before building voting mechanics.
- **Inside Burlington:** requires genuine access and original photography.

## Editorial rule for this backlog

A high score is permission to report, not permission to publish. If research breaks the premise, change the premise. If the evidence cannot support the headline, hold it. If a better story appears in the documents, follow the better story.
