# Video Leads — editorial rules

This pipeline finds questions. It does not write Burlington News articles.

## Hard rules

- Do not turn a transcript into a story.
- Do not auto-publish.
- Do not treat a creator claim as a Burlington News fact.
- Credit the creator if a video materially inspired the reporting.
- Use the official YouTube embed if the video is shown. Do not rehost it.
- Do not use a paused video frame as a hero image unless rights allow it. Prefer an official image, an original photo, a map, or a labelled Burlington News illustration.

## Lead types

- **Idea lead** — preferred. The video pointed at a question. The article is independent reporting.
- **Creator investigation** — attribute the discovery, then verify.
- **Worth watching** — the video is better than a derivative article. Keep it in the queue. Do not force a story.

## Slow news day

Use this order. Never publish a weak derivative because Burlington was quiet.

1. Breaking Burlington
2. Major Burlington original
3. New Burlington reporting
4. Useful Burlington evergreen
5. Strong Halton / Hamilton / Oakville story
6. Major Toronto / Ontario story with Burlington relevance
7. Video-discovered independently reported explainer
8. Hidden Burlington / history / development backlog
9. Worth Watching

## Worth Watching

If this ever becomes a public module it should be a short curated item: title, one or two sentences, official embed, creator credit, link. It should not be auto-added from the queue.
