Burlington Election 2026: Compare the mayoral candidates

Five names are on the official mayor list. Only three have published enough to compare in detail.

Direct answer

The official 2026 mayor candidates are Marianne Meed Ward, Lisa Kearns, Rory Nisan, Keith Demoe and Yugalkeesor Ramdhonee. Meed Ward is running on continuity and funded services. Kearns and Nisan want slower city spending growth and less use of extra mayoral power. Ramdhonee focuses on tax transparency and seniors. No detailed 2026 platform for Demoe has been found. Online voting starts Oct. 14. Election Day is Oct. 26.

By Burlington News Staff · Published Aug. 26, 2026 · Updated Aug. 26, 2026

Major differences

  • Continuity versus a slower budget: Meed Ward argues delaying repairs costs more later. Kearns and Nisan say the recent increases need a harder value test.
  • Mayoral power: Kearns says she would lead without relying on Strong Mayor tools. Nisan wants more decisions left with council. Meed Ward says she has avoided the most controversial veto-style tools.
  • Who has a record to inspect: Three candidates have sat on this council. Two have not. Demoe’s last public electoral record is a 2022 Ward 2 result: 653 votes, 10.53%.

Unanswered questions

  • Meed Ward: What did residents get for recent tax increases, and what changes in a third term?
  • Kearns: What would she cut, delay or redesign, and how much would that save?
  • Nisan: How would infrastructure stay funded if spending grows more slowly?
  • Demoe: Where can voters read any 2026 platform?
  • Ramdhonee: What would the seniors and housing proposals cost, and which government pays?
  • All five: What would they do about Halton-level crime trends the mayor does not control?

Why it matters in Burlington

The mayor proposes the budget and now holds extra Strong Mayor powers. Council still votes on most final decisions. Halton Region and the school boards set other parts of the tax bill. A campaign promise is not a completed outcome.
