2026 municipal election

What ward am I in?

The 2026 map moved several neighbourhoods. Enter your address in the City’s official finder, then use the ward cards below.

Direct answer

Burlington has six wards. Your councillor race and school-board trustee contests depend on that ward. The official lookup is the City of Burlington’s 2026 Find My Ward map. Online voting starts Oct. 14. Election Day is Oct. 26. Confirm the final ballot at myvote.burlington.ca.

What changed for 2026

  • North Tyandaga moves from Ward 1 to Ward 3.
  • Leighland moves from Ward 2 to Ward 1.
  • Palmer moves from Ward 4 to Ward 3.
  • Maple Beach moves from Ward 1 to Ward 2.
  • Wards 5 and 6 are unchanged.

Councillor and trustee contests

Names come from the City’s official candidate list, last checked Aug. 23, 2026. Trustee eligibility depends on school-board support. French-language trustees are citywide: Sonia Macaluso (Viamonde) and Diane Courchesne (MonAvenir).
