Burlington Farmers Market

A recurring Lions Club market at Burlington Centre, not a one-off weekend listing.

When it runs

Regular market: Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays, 7:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m., May 20 through October 24, 2026. Additional Wednesday evening market: 2:30 to 7:30 p.m., June 3 through September 30, 2026. Those are separate sessions, not one 7 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. market.
