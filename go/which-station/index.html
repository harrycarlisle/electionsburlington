Getting around

Which GO station should I use?

The closest station is not always the one with a parking space at 7:40 a.m.

Direct answer

Use Aldershot for west Burlington, Waterdown Road / 403 access and VIA connections. Use Burlington GO for the centre of the city, a parking garage and bike rooms. Use Appleby for east Burlington and the largest free parking supply. All three have free customer parking and Burlington Transit connections.

Road access

Aldershot sits on Waterdown Road near the 403 / QEW split. Burlington GO is on Fairview between Brant and Guelph Line. Appleby is on Fairview at Appleby Line. Train service on Lakeshore West serves all three; frequency still depends on the day and direction. Check the Burlington → Union board for the next valid departure.
