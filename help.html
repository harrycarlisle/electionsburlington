Help & feedback

Found an error, have a better source, need an accessibility fix or think something important is missing? Send it in.

Report an error or suggest an improvement

Use the feedback form for incorrect or outdated information, a broken link, missing context, an accessibility problem, a source suggestion or a feature request.

Give feedback

Please do not include private or sensitive information that is not needed to resolve the issue.

For candidates and public officials

If something attributed to you is inaccurate, identify the exact page and wording and provide the strongest available source. A campaign statement can establish your position; an official record is preferable when the dispute concerns a vote, filing, decision or other verifiable event.

Correction and removal requests are reviewed consistently. A request alone does not guarantee removal.

Accessibility

The site is intended to work with keyboard navigation, browser zoom, screen readers and reduced-motion settings. It includes a skip link, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings and navigation, and light/dark appearance controls.

If a page, comparison, form or source link is difficult to use with assistive technology, report the page and what happened. Accessibility issues are treated as bugs.

How the guide is built

Public candidate information, official records, reputable local reporting and clearly labelled public discussion are kept separate. Automated monitoring can flag changes and collect possible updates, but higher-risk claims and candidate records require source review before publication.
