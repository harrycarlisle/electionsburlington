BurlingtonElection Guide

Independent

This is an independent civic project. It is not run by the City of Burlington, a political party, a candidate or a campaign.

Why it exists

I built this because I wanted to understand the choices in Burlington's municipal election before deciding how to vote. Candidate platforms, council records and local issues can be spread across many sources and can be difficult to compare. The goal of this site is to make that information easier to understand without telling anyone who to support.

How candidates are treated

  • No endorsements or preferred candidates.
  • The same basic structure is used for each candidate: priorities, record, open questions and sources.
  • Claims are linked back to campaign material, public records, official sources or reporting where possible.
  • When information is missing, the site says that it was not found rather than guessing.

Corrections

Information can change, especially during an election. If something is wrong, outdated, missing context or linked incorrectly, use Give feedback. Important voting details should always be confirmed with the City of Burlington.

What this site is not

It is not an official election website, legal advice, polling, a campaign, or a substitute for the City's official voting information.
