BurlingtonElection Guide
About this project

Independent

This is a Burlington resident's civic-information project. It is not run by the City, a political party, a candidate or a campaign.

Why I made it

I wanted to understand Burlington's municipal election before deciding how to vote. The useful information was spread across campaign sites, council records, government pages, interviews and local discussion, and it was harder than it should be to understand what each position would actually mean.

The goal is to do that reading once, link the evidence and explain the important part in normal language so other residents can make up their own minds.

How candidates are treated

  • No endorsements or preferred candidates.
  • The same basic structure is used: overview, priorities, experience and record, questions worth checking and sources.
  • Campaign promises are labelled as promises. Public records are used for things that can be independently checked.
  • Positive and critical public comments can be included when they raise a substantive point, but the factual part is checked separately.
  • If something cannot be verified, the guide says so instead of filling the gap with an assumption.

Corrections

If something is wrong, outdated or missing important context, use Give feedback and include the page and strongest source you have. Important voting details should always be confirmed with the City of Burlington.

What this is not

It is not an official election website, polling, legal advice or a campaign. It is a source-linked guide intended to make local civic information easier to understand.
