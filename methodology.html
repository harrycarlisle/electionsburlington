Sources & methodology

How information gets onto the site, what is automated, and what is not.

Source order

When sources disagree, we generally prefer the most direct record.

  • Official records: City of Burlington, Halton Region, election records, council votes and formal decisions.
  • Candidate sources: candidate websites, public statements and campaign material for what a candidate says they support.
  • Local reporting: established reporting such as BurlingtonToday for interviews, context and events.
  • Community discussion: useful for identifying questions and themes, but not treated as polling or proof of a factual allegation.

Burlington in 30 seconds

An automated monitor checks a defined group of Burlington civic sources several times a day. It looks for changes involving City Hall, elections, budgets, taxes, development, transit, housing, council decisions and other local issues.

Items are ranked by civic importance. The system does not generate candidate endorsements, popularity scores or political conclusions.

5Critical: voting deadlines, election results, major formal decisions with immediate citywide impact.4Major: voting windows, budgets, major tax changes, major development or transit decisions.3Important: debates, significant candidate/platform updates, council votes and major public meetings.2Useful: relevant local political updates with narrower impact.1Reference: lower-priority background information.

“Why it matters” is normally shown only for higher-importance items where there is a concrete consequence to explain.

Candidate information

Candidate names and ballot placement come from the City of Burlington's official candidate list. Policy summaries should link back to a candidate source or public record wherever possible.

Candidate images are added only when the identity match is high-confidence. The automated photo checker first uses candidate-supplied websites, then exact-name local candidate profiles. If it cannot verify the person, the site keeps an initials placeholder rather than guessing.

Ward lookup

This site does not calculate a ward from a postal code. Burlington changed parts of Wards 1–4 for the 2026 election, so the ward finder sends the exact address to the City of Burlington's official interactive map.

Images

Images must be relevant to the specific story or person. Generic images are not inserted just to fill space. Duplicate images should not be repeated across several recent briefing items.

Where a third-party image is used, the page links back to the underlying source. Copyright and licensing can vary by source, so higher-risk or unclear images should be omitted rather than assumed to be reusable.

Corrections

If a fact is wrong, outdated or misleadingly summarized, use Give feedback and include the page plus a source. Corrections can be made without deleting the historical source record.
