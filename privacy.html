Privacy policy

The guide is designed to work without an account and to collect as little visitor information as practical.

What the site stores

The guide does not require an account and does not create advertising or political-preference profiles. A small setting may be stored in your browser so the site remembers light or dark mode.

The ward finder on this site uses the City of Burlington's official tool. If you enter an address into that embedded City tool, the request is handled by the City/ArcGIS service rather than by this site's own database.

Feedback

The feedback page lets you write a message and optionally enter an email address. The current form opens your own email application with that information filled in; the static website does not submit or store the form contents itself.

If you send the email, your email provider and the receiving mail service will process the message under their own privacy practices. Do not include sensitive information that is not needed to resolve the issue.

Hosting and technical information

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and related network infrastructure may process ordinary technical information needed to deliver a webpage, such as IP address, browser information, request time and requested page. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

External and embedded services

The guide links to government pages, news organizations, candidate websites, public records and social platforms. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Embedded third-party services, such as the official City ward map, may receive ordinary browser and request information when they load. Where practical, the guide links directly to the original service as an alternative.

Changes

If a future feature materially changes what information the guide collects or stores, this page should be updated before or when that feature is introduced.
