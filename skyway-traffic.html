How the traffic status works

Burlington News checks the official Ontario 511 traffic-events API for incidents near the Skyway and, when the browser can safely read the live camera frames, makes a lightweight visual estimate from the current images. The visual estimate is a convenience, not an official traffic measurement. For closures, collisions and roadwork, Ontario 511 is the source of record. Camera and event data are Ontario Ministry of Transportation data under the Open Government Licence - Ontario.
