What this can,and can’t,tell you

The pictures are live. Ontario 511 publishes the three camera feeds and refreshes its images frequently.

The route buttons are live too. Google Maps supplies current travel time after you open a route. Burlington News doesn’t collect your trip.

For collisions, closures and roadwork, use the Ontario 511 map. Don’t use this page while driving.
