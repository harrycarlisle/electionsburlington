Which Burlington neighbourhood has the best small park?

We compared compact Burlington parks by what they actually let a neighbourhood do in a small amount of space.

By Burlington NewsAugust 29, 20263 min read
Topics:Parks

Aldershot's Bolus Gardens Parkette is one of the strongest answers. The site packs a playground, splash pad, basketball court, ball-hockey pad, pickleball lines, gardens and a connection to the Francis Road Bikeway into a neighbourhood-scale park.

The city also added Bolus Gardens to its new free sports-equipment locker program in 2026, so visitors can borrow equipment at the park instead of bringing everything from home.

Why Bolus Gardens stands out

Most small parks specialize. Some are mainly a playground. Some are a patch of green space. Some exist to provide one specific amenity.

Bolus Gardens combines several different uses. Burlington's 2026 capital budget describes it as having a playground, basketball court, ball-hockey pad, horticultural gardens and a walkway connected to the Francis Road Bikeway. The city also lists a splash pad there and two pickleball-lined courts inside the ball-hockey area.

That mix makes it useful to more than one age group at the same time.

There are better small parks for specific things

If the goal is a small dog park, Colin Alton Parkette has a stronger case. The city lists it as an official leash-free area, and a 2023 city report says a new playground, leash-free dog park and outdoor fitness equipment were completed there in 2021.

If the goal is a quiet lake view, Burlington's Windows-to-the-Lake sites are difficult to beat. These are deliberately small shoreline parks with paths and accessible seating. The city describes them as places to sit, watch the water and see sunsets over Lake Ontario.

So which neighbourhood wins?

For the most amenities in one compact everyday park, Aldershot wins this comparison with Bolus Gardens Parkette.

That does not make it Burlington's best park overall. It makes it a useful example of what a small neighbourhood park can do when the space is asked to serve several different activities instead of one.
