A Burlington team just went 0–24. How does that even happen?

Zero wins. Twenty-four losses. The next game is not a chance to repair that record.

By Burlington NewsAugust 26, 20264 min read

The Burlington Ultimate Club team called Hotspots finished the last two completed regular seasons with no wins.

That is 0–24: 12 losses last season, 12 losses this season. Not 24–0. They did not win the games. The other teams did.

The record, plainly
  • Team: Hotspots
  • Club: Burlington Ultimate Club
  • Last two regular seasons: 0 wins, 24 losses
  • Highest score in a game: 7
  • Games play to 15

The scoreline is the story

Ultimate games in this league play to 15. A team that never got past 7 was not close on the scoreboard, even when it was still on the field.

That does not make the players a joke. It makes the record unusual enough that a Burlington reader can understand it without a league explainer.

The playoff game does not change 0–24

Hotspots have an upcoming playoff game. That game sits outside the regular-season standings.

A win there would be a real result. It would not rewrite the 0–24 regular-season line.

Why this is a local story

Burlington has plenty of winning teams. This is the other end of the same civic sports map: a club side that kept showing up after the result was no longer in doubt.

The useful question is not whether 0–24 is embarrassing. It is what a rec-league season is for when the scoreboard never turns.

Read the line as written: 0 wins, 24 losses. A playoff game is a new game, not a correction to the regular season.
