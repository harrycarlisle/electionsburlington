What the Nelson Quarry decision means for Burlington.

The City opposed the proposed Mount Nemo expansion. The tribunal decision means Burlington now has to assess the conditions, next approvals and any remaining legal options.

Burlington News · Aug. 24, 2026

The Ontario Land Tribunal issued a decision on the proposed Nelson Quarry expansion at Mount Nemo on Aug. 18. Burlington City Council had opposed the expansion over concerns that included groundwater, wildlife, the Niagara Escarpment and nearby rural communities.

What happens now

The City says staff and legal counsel are reviewing the full decision. That review matters because a tribunal decision can still contain conditions, staging requirements, monitoring obligations and additional approval steps before a project moves ahead.

Why Burlington residents should care

The quarry sits in north Burlington near the Mount Nemo plateau. The case affects questions that go beyond a single site: how growth is balanced against groundwater protection, natural heritage and the long-term character of rural Burlington.
