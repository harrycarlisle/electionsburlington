What does this mean for me?

Burlington property taxes: why did my bill change?

The City, Halton Region and the school boards each take a slice. Only one of those slices is the mayor’s budget.

Direct answer

The 2026 urban residential bill rose 4.50%, or $43.71 for every $100,000 of current value assessment. The City budget rose 5.81%, which is 2.98 percentage points of the household increase. Halton’s budget rose 4.60%, or 1.51 points. Education rates did not increase. A $800,000 home at the City’s published rates pays about $349.68 more than in 2025.
