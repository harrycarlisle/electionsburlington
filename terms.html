Terms of use

The guide summarizes public information to make Burlington civic issues easier to understand. Original sources remain the best place to verify an important fact.

What this site is

The Burlington Election Guide is an independent project created by a Burlington resident. It is not the City of Burlington, Halton Region, Elections Ontario, the Province of Ontario, a political party, candidate or campaign.

The site does not endorse candidates and is not an official source for eligibility, voting locations, legal requirements or government decisions.

For voting information: confirm deadlines, eligibility, locations and your final ballot with the City of Burlington.

Accuracy and sourcing

Content may summarize government records, candidate statements, interviews, reporting and public discussion. Summaries can simplify context, source pages can change and mistakes are possible.

We try to keep verified records, campaign claims, opinion and unresolved allegations separate. When a factual claim cannot be verified, it should be labelled accordingly rather than presented as established fact.

Use and reliance

The site is provided for general civic information and is not legal, financial, planning, voting or other professional advice. You are responsible for checking official information and making your own decisions.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the site is provided “as is” and “as available” without a guarantee that every item is complete, current or error-free.

Corrections and disputes

If a factual statement is wrong, materially incomplete, outdated or incorrectly attributed, use the feedback form and include the page, disputed wording and strongest available source.

Requests are reviewed for accuracy, sourcing, public relevance, privacy, safety, fairness and applicable law. A request does not automatically require removal. When relevant to the public record, the site may note that a candidate, campaign or public official requested a correction or removal and explain the outcome without publishing unnecessary private information.

Links are provided so readers can inspect sources. Third-party sites control their own material and may change or remove it. Linking does not imply endorsement.

Names, public statements, government records and limited third-party material may be referenced for reporting, commentary, comparison and civic-information purposes. Rights in third-party material remain with their respective owners.
