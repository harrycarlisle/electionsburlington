    How this page works

    Incidents come from the official Ontario 511 events feed and are rewritten into a location, direction and nearby interchange. Route cameras are selected by mapping official camera coordinates to an OpenStreetMap driving route. Traffic density is labelled “looks” only when a scheduled estimate exists. It is not an official speed reading. Ontario 511 remains the source of record for closures and collisions.
