What changed for 2026?

  • North Tyandaga moves from Ward 1 to Ward 3.

  • Palmer moves from Ward 4 to Ward 3.
  • Maple Beach moves from Ward 1 to Ward 2.
  • Wards 5 and 6 are unchanged.

These boundaries are for the 2026 municipal election and take effect for the new council term. If the interactive map and a neighbourhood description seem to conflict, rely on the exact-address result from the City.
